# ANDHRA PRADESH GENERAL ELECTIONS 2008 - COMPREHENSIVE REPORT

## Election Commission of India
### Statistical Report and Electoral Analysis
### Andhra Pradesh Legislative Assembly Elections 2008 (Undivided State)

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The 2008 Andhra Pradesh Legislative Assembly Elections were held on November 24, 2008 and December 1, 2008 (two phases). This was during Y.S. Rajasekhar Reddy's second term as Chief Minister.

**Key Highlights:**
- Total Assembly Seats: 294 (Undivided AP)
- Total Lok Sabha Seats: 42
- Election Phases: Two phases (November-December 2008)
- Total Registered Voters: 67.2 Million
- Votes Polled: 38.5 Million
- Voter Turnout: 57.3%
- Election Winner: Indian National Congress
- Chief Minister: Y.S. Rajasekhar Reddy (continued)
- Results Declared: December 5, 2008

---

## PART 2: POLITICAL CONTEXT - 2008

### 2.1 Y.S. Rajasekhar Reddy Era

**Background:**
- Chief Minister: Y.S. Rajasekhar Reddy (1952-2009)
- First Term: 2004-2009
- Second Term: 2009-2014 (won in 2008)

**Notable Policies (2004-2008):**

- Groundnut farming challenges

**3. Regional Disparities**
- Telangana vs Andhra vs Rayalaseema

## PART 4: KEY ELECTORAL ISSUES - 2008

### 4.1 Telangana Statehood (Critical - 45% of voters)

**Background:**
- Movement for separate Telangana state
- TRS leading agitation
- Congress's vague position
- TDP's opposition

**Congress's Stance:**

- Eventually (2014): Bifurcation approved

- TRS boycott reduced competition in Telangana
- Congress benefited in Andhra regions
- Voting pattern fragmented in Telangana

## PART 8: Y.S. RAJASEKHAR REDDY'S TENURE

### 8.1 Major Achievements (2004-2009)

1. **Free Electricity for Farmers**
   - Hugely popular scheme
   - Cost: ₹8,000 crores/year
   - Became model for other states

2. **Education Scholarships**
   - Vidyalakshmi scheme
   - Free education for poor students
   - Benefited millions

3. **Healthcare Programs**
   - Aarogyasri scheme (health insurance)
   - Free treatment for poor
   - Rural health centers

4. **Infrastructure Development**
   - IT corridor development (Hyderabad)
   - Port development (Visakhapatnam)
   - Highway projects

---

### 8.2 Tragic Death (May 25, 2009)

- Y.S. Rajasekhar Reddy died in helicopter crash
- During second term
- Major political shock
- Succession issue
- Led to instability (2009-2014)
- His son Y.S. Jagan Mohan Reddy later founded YSRCP

---

## PART 9: IMPACT AND LEGACY

### 9.1 Policy Impact

**Free Electricity for Farmers:**
- Became template for other states
- Increased agricultural dependence on government
- Massive fiscal burden
- But hugely popular

**Education Scholarships:**
- Improved school enrollment
- Helped SC/ST students
- Expensive scheme
- Later expanded/modified

**Healthcare (Aarogyasri):**
- Set model for health insurance
- Later replicated nationally (Ayushman Bharat)
- Covered millions

---

### 9.2 Road to Bifurcation (2008-2014)

**Post-2008 Events:**
- YS Rajasekhar Reddy's death (May 2009)
- Leadership instability
- Telangana agitation continues (2009-2013)
- TRS leading movement
- Finally, Congress approves bifurcation (2013)
- Bifurcation takes effect (June 2, 2014)

---

### 9.3 YSRCP Foundation (2011)

- Y.S. Jagan Mohan Reddy (son of late CM) founds YSRCP
- Builds on YS Rajasekhar Reddy's legacy
- Promises: Free electricity, education, welfare
- Later wins 2019 elections with 151 seats
- Becomes Chief Minister 2019-2024

---

## CONCLUSION

The 2008 Andhra Pradesh General Elections took place during a period of welfare-oriented governance under Y.S. Rajasekhar Reddy. The Congress victory was driven largely by populist schemes and the CM's personal popularity. However, the election also reflected growing regional tensions, particularly the Telangana statehood movement, which would eventually lead to the state's bifurcation in 2014.

The era (2004-2009) established welfare politics as central to AP elections, a trend that continues to influence electoral outcomes even today.
